# Project EARLY — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0 (Hackathon MVP Specification)  
**Product Name:** Project EARLY (*Preventive Care Intelligence*)  
**Tagline:** *Every health record tells a story. EARLY connects it.*  
**Business Model:** B2B2C (Clinics, Hospitals & Diagnostic Centres as paying customers; Patients as connected end users)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

Project EARLY is a connected preventive-care platform with **one central patient health record** and **two role-based experiences**:
1. **Patient Dashboard:** A simple, personal, plain-language portal where patients view their multi-year medical history, chronological health timeline, lab reports, treatment history, upcoming follow-ups, and easy-to-understand early-signal prompts.
2. **Doctor & Hospital Dashboard:** A clinical workspace where authorised doctors and clinic staff access consolidated patient histories, review and verify uploaded reports, monitor longitudinal biomarker trends against clinician-approved deterministic rules, record encounters and treatments, and track preventive follow-ups to completion.

**Core Mission:** Connect a patient's past medical records with their present healthcare needs so preventive follow-ups do not fall through the cracks.

### Clinical & Product Boundary (Non-Negotiable)
- Project EARLY is **not** a diagnostic engine, **not** a disease-prediction model (e.g., heart attack or cancer prediction), and **not** an autonomous prescribing tool.
- AI/OCR is used strictly to **extract and organise** unstructured medical reports into structured records, which remain subject to **human-in-the-loop verification**.
- Clinical alerts ("Early Signals") are generated by **deterministic, clinician-approved, guideline-based rules**—never by an LLM making a diagnosis.

## 2. Problem Statement & Validation

### 2.1 The Core Problem
Patients frequently visit multiple doctors, clinics, and diagnostic laboratories over several years. Their reports, prescriptions, and risk indicators remain scattered across paper files, emails, and disconnected hospital systems. Meanwhile, clinicians operate in compressed consultations (often ~4–5 minutes) without a connected longitudinal view or a reliable workflow to ensure recommended preventive follow-ups (such as repeat blood pressure checks or HbA1c re-testing) actually happen.

### 2.2 Market & Evidence Context (India)
- **Screening vs. Continuity Gap:** India's National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases (NP-NCD) has conducted massive screenings (over 33.53 crore hypertension screenings and 32.18 crore diabetes screenings reported by July 2025), yet multicentric assessments highlight persistent gaps in revisit rates, treatment adherence, and disease control.
- **Fragmented Care:** Primary healthcare studies in India document insufficient retention of clinical information and fragmented service delivery as major barriers to continuity of care.
- **Role of Clinical Decision Support:** Systematic reviews of randomised trials show clinical decision-support systems improve preventive care delivery, while longitudinal multi-timepoint decision support remains an underserved opportunity.
- **ABDM Market Reality:** With India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) surpassing 100 crore linked health records, Project EARLY does **not** claim to be the first platform to store or share medical records. Instead, EARLY turns existing patient records into an **actionable preventive-care and follow-up workflow**.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 4. Value Proposition & Differentiation

### 4.1 Combined Value Proposition
- **For Patients:** *"Understand your health journey, access your medical history, and stay informed about your care."*
- **For Doctors & Hospitals:** *"Access connected patient information, monitor changes over time, and manage preventive follow-ups."*

### 4.2 Differentiation Against Existing Solutions (Eka Care, Practo Ray, HealthPlix, ABDM)
1. **Complete Longitudinal Journey:** Organises reports, diagnoses, and treatments into a unified chronological timeline rather than isolated document folders.
2. **Dual-Perspective Shared Record:** One central source of truth rendered in plain language for patients and clinical detail for doctors.
3. **Longitudinal Comparison:** Compares historical biomarkers across dates to surface changes requiring professional review.
4. **Closed-Loop Preventive Follow-Ups:** Links clinical findings directly to tracked follow-up actions (Pending, Overdue, Completed, Postponed, Cancelled).

## 5. Primary Hackathon Use Case

**Focus Domain:** **Hypertension and Diabetes Preventive Follow-Up**
- **Tracked Biomarkers (MVP):** Systolic/Diastolic Blood Pressure (mmHg), Fasting/Random Blood Glucose (mg/dL), HbA1c (%), plus optional baseline lipid/renal markers (LDL Cholesterol, eGFR) where present in uploaded reports.
- **Illustrative Workflow:**
  1. A 48-year-old patient visits Clinic A; BP is recorded at 146/92 mmHg and reassessment is advised, but the follow-up never happens.
  2. Months later, the patient visits Clinic B (or another doctor).
  3. EARLY surfaces the prior elevated BP, the unfinished reassessment, and recent lab trends in the clinician's view.
  4. The clinician reviews the history, records an encounter/treatment update, and schedules or closes the follow-up.

## 6. Functional Requirements

### 6.1 P0 — Hackathon MVP Scope

| ID | Feature | User Role(s) | Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FR-01** | **Authentication & RBAC** | All | Secure registration and login with role-based access (`PATIENT`, `DOCTOR`, `CLINIC_ADMIN`, `STAFF`). |
| **FR-02** | **Patient & Clinic Profiles** | Patient, Doctor, Admin | Manage patient demographics, clinic affiliation, and authorised patient directory search. |
| **FR-03** | **Consent & Record Sharing** | Patient, Doctor | Patients grant or revoke clinic/doctor access to their central health record. Doctors can only view authorised patients. |
| **FR-04** | **Medical Report Upload** | Patient, Doctor, Staff | Upload PDF or image lab reports; store original file immutably with upload timestamp and source provider metadata. |
| **FR-05** | **AI/OCR Structured Extraction & Human Verification** | Doctor, Staff, Patient | Python OCR + LLM extracts test name, value, unit, reference range, date, and confidence score into structured JSON. UI presents extracted values side-by-side with the source document for manual verification/correction before committing to the verified timeline. |
| **FR-06** | **Longitudinal Medical Timeline** | Patient, Doctor | Chronological, year-and-date-grouped timeline combining verified lab reports, biomarker observations, encounters, and treatments. Never silently overwrite historical entries. |
| **FR-07** | **Biomarker Trend Visualisation** | Patient, Doctor | Interactive Recharts line/trend charts comparing selected biomarkers (e.g., HbA1c, BP, Glucose) over time with reference bands. |
| **FR-08** | **Deterministic Early-Signal Rules Engine** | Doctor, Patient | Clinician-approved deterministic rules evaluate verified observations (e.g., elevated BP without completed reassessment, overdue follow-up, missing required baseline value, significant longitudinal drift). Displays clinical review flags to doctors and plain-language prompts to patients. |
| **FR-09** | **Encounter & Treatment History** | Doctor, Patient | Doctors record consultations, diagnoses, medications/treatments, and clinical notes; patients view past treatments clearly. |
| **FR-10** | **Preventive Follow-Up Management** | Doctor, Staff, Patient | Clinicians create follow-up tasks (action type, due date, notes). Staff/clinicians transition status (`PENDING`, `COMPLETED`, `OVERDUE`, `POSTPONED`, `CANCELLED`). Patients view upcoming/pending tests and appointments. |
| **FR-11** | **Clinic Operational Dashboard** | Doctor, Clinic Admin | Displays workflow metrics: total registered patients, follow-ups due, overdue follow-ups, completed follow-ups, and patients needing clinician review. |
| **FR-12** | **Audit Logging & Correction Requests** | Admin, Patient | Immutable audit trail of report uploads, record views, value edits, consent changes, and follow-up updates. Patients can submit correction requests for inaccurate records. |

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Security & Privacy:** Role-based access control (RBAC), patient consent enforcement on every API request, TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, PII de-identification prior to external LLM inference, and append-only audit logs.
- **Regulatory Alignment:** Designed for alignment with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act**, **ABDM** consent principles, and HIPAA/GDPR security best practices.
- **Clinical Safety & Language:** Patient UI must avoid alarming diagnostic terminology; every early-signal prompt must state that it is informational and advise consulting a healthcare professional.
- **Data Provenance:** Every extracted biomarker must link back to its `source_report_id`, `observed_at` date, source lab/clinic, and verification actor.
- **Demo Data Policy:** Hackathon environment must use strictly **synthetic/fictional patient histories**—never real identifiable patient records.

## 8. Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope for Hackathon)

- Autonomous AI disease diagnosis or medication/dosage recommendations.
- Heart attack, stroke, or cancer risk prediction claims.
- Generic conversational health chatbot.
- Environmental pollution dashboards or wearable-device integrations.
- Live production hospital EHR/HIS or production ABDM integrations.

## 9. Success Metrics & Hackathon Demonstration Plan

### 9.1 MVP Evaluation Metrics (Measured on Labelled Synthetic Dataset)
- **Extraction Accuracy:** Percentage of test names, numeric values, units, and dates accurately extracted from sample PDFs/images.
- **Timeline Accuracy:** Correct chronological ordering and preservation of dates/units across multiple providers.
- **Rule Accuracy:** Precision of deterministic rules in flagging configured follow-up and review conditions.
- **Follow-Up Closure:** End-to-end creation, tracking, and status completion of follow-up tasks.
- **Review Efficiency:** Reduction in manual PDF review steps during simulated consultation prep.

### 9.2 Hackathon Demo Script
1. **Seed 3 Fictional Patient Histories** (e.g., chronic hypertension with missed reassessment, pre-diabetic HbA1c upward drift, and routine stable follow-up).
2. **Upload a New Unstructured Lab PDF** -> demonstrate OCR/AI extraction -> human verification & correction -> commit to timeline.
3. **Show Both Views:**
   - **Doctor Dashboard:** Triage queue highlighting the patient needing review, multi-year biomarker chart, deterministic early-signal flag, and follow-up creation/completion.
   - **Patient Dashboard:** Plain-language health timeline, treatment history, and clear upcoming follow-up card.
